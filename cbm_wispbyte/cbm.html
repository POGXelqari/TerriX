Clan Loan Policy & Terms

Mathematical principles that govern clan capital allocation and defend against bad debt or insolvency.

Automated Risk Engine
⚖️

1. Reserve-Backed Ceiling (0.05%)

No single loan facility may exceed 0.05% of the bank's unencumbered central reserves. Member deposited liabilities are segregated and strictly excluded from loan pools.

🔒

2. 1,000 Gold Activation Floor

If 0.05% of the unencumbered reserves does not exceed 1,000 Gold, lending is locked completely. This establishes a required reserve floor of 2,000,000 Gold before any credit facility opens.

📅

3. 14-Day Return Window (0% Rate)

Active loans carry 0% interest during the initial 14 days. Borrowers have a guaranteed 14-day window to return principal without incurring fee or financing penalties.

⚠️

4. 50% Penalty Interest on Default

Loans unpaid after 14 days automatically transition to OVERDUE status and trigger a fixed 50% penalty interest rate assessed across the loan principal.

🛡️

5. 20 Gold Safe Buffer Protection

When delinquent loans are garnished from account balance, the engine always preserves a minimum 20.00 Gold buffer so the member's account is never purged by nightly game balance maintenance.

🚫

6. Downgraded Access & Interception

If balance is insufficient to clear the debt, account role is downgraded to Restricted (withdrawals suspended). Inbound deposits are automatically intercepted until debt is 0, restoring full membership.

🔗

7. Multi-Credential Deposit Pooling

Multiple in-game accounts and credentials can be linked to the same CBM account. Inbound deposits from any linked alt or main account pool into a single balance and can be used interchangeably for loan repayments or unified withdrawals.
