Clan Bank Manager Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 • Applies to: cbm.wispbyte.org, Developer API, and CBM Services • Version: 2.4
🔒 Privacy & Non-Custodial Architecture Overview
Clan Bank Manager (CBM) operates an automated, sovereign treasury and ledger synchronization protocol for the Territorial.io gaming community. We never collect, store, or ask for your Territorial.io game passwords, emails, or personal payment card credentials. All ledger settlements are anchored exclusively in verifiable public match transfer logs and player-selected cryptographic authentication PINs.
Section 1

Information We Process and Retain

To maintain an honest, high-speed, and secure ledger of player deposits, loans, and clan sponsorship rewards, CBM processes a strictly limited set of game and security signals:

Data Category Specific Information Purpose & Operational Need
Public Game Telemetry In-game player handles, clan tags, match deposit transactions, public ledger transfer hashes, and match timestamps. Verifying inbound Gold deposits to vault player handle DdcBC and attributing clan balance balances.
Account Credentials Chosen CBM username, cryptographic salted PIN hash (PBKDF2/SHA-256), optional hashed login password, and optional avatar URL. Authorizing balance transfers, withdrawal intent declarations, loan repayments, and CBM Plus subscriptions.
Security & Anti-Abuse Client IP address (anonymized in application logs), Cloudflare Turnstile human-verification tokens, and API rate-limit counters. Preventing automated credential stuffing, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS), and bot exploitation.
Local Client State Browser localStorage and sessionStorage tokens (e.g. cbm_session_token, cbm_user). Maintaining member session persistence across page refreshes. Zero third-party cross-site tracking cookies.
Section 2

Telemetry, Analytics & Google Tag Manager

CBM utilizes Google Tag Manager (container ID G-QCMCPSK0CF) strictly for aggregate performance analytics, Core Web Vitals profiling, and technical error diagnosis:

  • Aggregated Visitor Metrics: We track high-level page visit frequency, device form factors (mobile vs desktop), and browser engine compatibility to optimize UI rendering speeds.
  • No Personal Profiling: We do not transmit game PINs, transaction balances, session tokens, or private member identifiers to advertising or cross-context tracking platforms.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): Script execution is strictly confined by server-enforced HTTP security headers allowing only authorized origins (https://www.googletagmanager.com, https://*.google-analytics.com, https://challenges.cloudflare.com).
Section 3

Cryptographic Security & Non-Custodial Safeguards

We employ state-of-the-art cryptographic architecture to protect all member balances and ledger records:

  • Zero Plaintext Storage: Member 4–8 digit PINs and optional passwords are salted using secure per-user salts and hashed with PBKDF2/SHA-256 prior to database storage. Plaintext credentials are never written to disk or recorded in debug logs.
  • Dual-Layer Database Replication: Operational transactions are settled atomically via local SQLite with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) and multi-threaded pooling, with encrypted remote disaster-recovery synchronization.
  • In-Memory Gzip Buffers: High-frequency telemetry endpoints leverage pre-compressed byte buffers in RAM, avoiding cold file-system traversal and minimizing CPU load.
Section 4

Third-Party Infrastructure & Service Providers

To maintain reliable uptime, edge caching, and bot mitigation, CBM coordinates with reputable enterprise infrastructure providers:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: Provides DDoS protection, edge TLS termination, Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA verification, and Zero-Trust tunnel ingress.
  • Google LLC: Provides site reliability analytics and Google Tag performance measurement.
  • Territorial.io Network: Public match lobbies and in-game transfer logs are polled by the CBM deposit reconciliation daemon to confirm Gold delivery.
Section 5

Member Rights, Data Export & Account Deletion

Every player maintains full autonomy over their CBM account information:

  • Transparency & Auditability: You can view all incoming deposits, loan disbursements, repayments, and CBM Plus subscription entries at any time on the public ledger and your member dashboard.
  • Credential Rotation: You may update your Quick Access PIN or password directly through authorized authentication endpoints.
  • Account Closure & Unlinking: If you wish to close your CBM account, disassociate your Territorial handle, or request permanent deletion of non-ledger metadata, submit a request via clan administration or the developer support channel. Note that completed public ledger entries (deposits and transfers) remain immutable for solvency audit integrity.
