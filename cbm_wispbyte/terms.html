Nature of Service & In-Game Virtual Currency

Clan Bank Manager operates an algorithmic, non-custodial bookkeeping ledger to track Gold contributions, clan loans, marketplace escrow, and war-chest reserves:

  • Virtual Currency Scope: "Gold" refers strictly to the in-game currency and points mechanism native to Territorial.io. Gold has no cash value, is not convertible into fiat currency, and is not an investment product, bank account, or security.
