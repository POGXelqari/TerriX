Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to some of the most common questions about Territorial.io.

I forgot my account password. How can I regain access to my account?

You can recover your password only if you previously registered an email address for your account. Email registration is available only for accounts with at least 100 Gold.
Open the Game Menu and select Account Recovery. Enter your 5-letter Account Name and the registered email address. Your password will be sent to that email if the account you are currently logged into has more than 2 Gold.
