Transactions

Any account can send gold to any other account. The transaction fee depends on overall transaction activity and whether a Bank is involved.

Check transfers at https://territorial.io/log/transactions.

On this page
  1. How it works
  2. Transaction fees

How it works

  1. Sender picks a receiver and an amount.
  2. The fee is charged to the sender; the receiver gets the full amount.

Transaction fees

Transaction fees help prevent spam. The fee can increase when there are many transactions and is capped at 2🧈. Banks receive an advantage on transaction fees.
