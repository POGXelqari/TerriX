Clan Bank Lending Constitution

The CBM loan facility is governed by strict mathematical risk controls to protect the clan treasury against insolvency and bad debt.

1. Reserve-Backed Ceiling (0.05%)

A loan cannot exceed 0.05% of the bank's unencumbered reserves. Member deposited liabilities are fully segregated and never lent out.

2. 1,000 Gold Activation Floor

If 0.05% of the bank's unencumbered reserves does not exceed 1,000 Gold, loans cannot be requested at all. This requires minimum 2,000,000 Gold in central reserves.

3. Automated Amortization

Approved loans have standard 14-day terms. Repayments are deducted automatically from any inbound member deposits until principal is settled.
