Propaganda War

This page explains how to launch a Propaganda Campaign from the in‑game Propaganda menu. Propaganda lets you promote things like your Discord or YouTube and influence the audience’s mood.

How Propaganda Campaigns Work

A Propaganda Campaign displays your message to players as paid impressions. The total Gold you invest funds these impressions. Every new campaign has a fixed 50 Gold fee that is deducted before the campaign can start. Plan for an investment strictly higher than 50 Gold and make sure your account has the necessary reserves.

Important: The audience can report propaganda. Poor or harmful text may increase the price per impression and severe violations can lead to account deletion.

Step‑by‑Step: Launch a Campaign

  1. Open the Propaganda menu in Territorial.io.
  2. Write your Propaganda Text (see guidelines below).
  3. (Optional) Set Colors to style the text and its window for recognizability.
  4. (Optional) Choose Targeting by language (at most one language; leave blank to reach all).
  5. Enter your Gold investment. Remember: 50 Gold is taken as a fee before the campaign begins.
  6. Review and Start the campaign.
  7. Visit territorial.io/log/propaganda to confirm that your campaign is running. The log may take a minute to refresh.

Write the Propaganda Text

  • Aim for a positive or neutral tone to keep costs low and avoid penalties.
  • Bad text can make the price per impression spike.
  • Extreme violations may result in the propagandist’s account being deleted.
  • You can format text with **bold** and *italic*.

Allowed Links (exact shapes)

If your text contains links in the exact formats below, they are recognized as clickable HTML links:

discord.gg/abc123
youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ
tiktok.com/@abc/video/123456789
t.me/username
x.com/username
patreon.com/username
Note: Only these domains and shapes are accepted. Use exactly the structure shown (no extra path, query, or fragments).

Colors (Branding)

You can set colors for the text and the window. Pick colors that fit your message or brand. Consistent colors improve recognition of your propaganda. There are a total of 11 color values that can be adjusted in the rgb(0–255) format: the first 7 control the background appearance of the propaganda window, while the last 4 determine the font color and how your message is displayed to the audience.

Targeting by Language

  • You can target at most one language. Leave it empty to target all languages.
  • Enter a language using a BCP 47 language tag (e.g., en, es).
  • Everything after a hyphen (-) is ignored. Example: pt-BR is treated as pt.
  • The Settings menu in Territorial.io shows languages in this format.

Gold Investment & Fees

  • 50 Gold is always deducted as fees when starting a new campaign (before it can run).
  • Your investment should be greater than 50 to fund impressions.
  • Ensure your account has sufficient Gold reserves.

Reports & Consequences

The audience can report propaganda. Poorly written or harmful campaigns can backfire by raising costs, reducing reach, or triggering moderation actions. For details, see Report War.

Tips & Best Practices

  • Keep your message clean, clear, and audience‑friendly.
  • Use one clear call‑to‑action.
  • Leverage bold and italic sparingly to highlight the essentials.
  • Match your colors to your brand or content (e.g., your Discord server theme).
  • Target a language only when it truly improves relevance.

Good Examples

  • Friendly tone: “Join our clan on Discord for weekly co‑op!”
  • Clear CTA: “Watch our latest match analysis on YouTube.”
  • Consistent colors: text/window colors matching your channel branding.
